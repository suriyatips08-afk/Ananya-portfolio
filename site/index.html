03 / ABOUT

PRODUCT DESIGN / RESEARCH / MAKING

I look closely, then make things matter.

I’m Ananya, a product design student interested in making everyday things more thoughtful, comfortable and useful. I move from observing how people use objects to sketching, modelling and testing ideas in my hands.

My work ranges from an ergonomic bed-table and a hair-curler redesign to material-led print studies and the hands-on making of an XPO auto-rickshaw installation. I enjoy the point where research meets real materials—and an idea becomes something people can use.
